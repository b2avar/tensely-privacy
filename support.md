# Tensely Support

For questions, problems or suggestions, write to us:

**info@tensemasteracademy.com**

We read messages every weekday and reply within **2 business days** at the
latest. If you are reporting a bug, telling us your device model, operating
system version and which screen the problem happened on lets us fix it much
faster.

---

## Frequently asked

### Is the app paid?

No, it is free to download. The first 5 lessons of every level are open even
without an account. A monthly or yearly subscription is needed to reach the
remaining lessons.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are cancelled through the store you bought them from, not from
inside the app.

**iPhone / iPad:** Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Tensely → Cancel Subscription

**Android:** Google Play app → your profile icon → Payments and subscriptions →
Subscriptions → Tensely → Cancel subscription

When you cancel, your premium access continues until the end of the period you
have already paid for.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to **Profile → Delete my account**. If you cannot reach the app,
write to us from your registered email address. See the
[account deletion section of the privacy policy](./en#account-deletion) for
details.

Note: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. You need to
cancel it separately using the steps above.

### Speaking exercises do not work

Speaking exercises ask for microphone permission. If you refused it, you can
grant Tensely microphone access from your device settings. If you would rather
not, that is fine — you can skip the speaking exercises and the rest of the
lesson works normally.

Some devices have no speech recognition service. In that case the app shows a
"this device does not support speech recognition" notice.

### Do speaking exercises grade my pronunciation?

No. We check whether what you said matches the expected sentence. There is no
pronunciation scoring.

### My progress disappeared

Progress is saved to the cloud if you created an account, and comes back when
you sign in with the same account. If you used the app without an account,
progress is kept only on that device and is lost if you delete the app.

### How do I change my level?

Tap the level name at the top of the **Learn** tab and pick the level you want
from the list. You can change it whenever you like; your progress is not erased.
