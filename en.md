# Tensely Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 August 2026
**Data controller:** TenseMaster Academy
**Contact:** info@tensemasteracademy.com

This policy explains which personal data the Tensely mobile application (the
"App") processes, for what purpose and how. It is written to meet the
requirements of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the
privacy policy requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

---

## 1. Personal Data We Process

### 1.1 Identity and contact
- Email address
- Name (optional)
- The unique user identifier shared by Apple or Google when you sign in with them
- Password (stored only as a hash on Supabase; Tensely cannot read it)

### 1.2 Learning data
- Chosen level and target proficiency
- Daily study goal in minutes
- Completed lessons, exercises attempted and their results
- Mastery scores and streak tracking
- Error analysis records (patterns in wrong answers)

### 1.3 Device and technical data
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Push notification token (only if you granted permission)

The app contains **no** crash reporting or analytics tool. If you have opted in,
the App Store and Google Play may collect anonymous crash data through their own
infrastructure; that data is subject to Apple's and Google's own policies and
never reaches us tied to your identity.

### 1.4 Microphone and speech recognition
In speaking exercises we use the microphone so we can show you whether the
English sentence you said matches the expected one.

- The microphone opens **only** when you press the record button in a speaking exercise. There is no background listening.
- Your speech is transcribed by **your operating system's own speech recognition service** (iOS Speech, Android SpeechRecognizer). These services may perform recognition on the device or on Apple's and Google's servers; your operating system decides which. That processing is subject to Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.
- **The audio never reaches Tensely's servers.** We neither record nor store it, and the transcribed text is discarded when the exercise ends.
- All that reaches Tensely is whether you completed the exercise correctly.
- You may refuse the permission. Speaking exercises are then skipped and the rest of the lesson works normally.

### 1.5 Subscription and payment
- Subscription status (active / cancelled / expired)
- Subscription type (monthly / yearly)
- Purchase dates
- **Payment details (card number, bank information and the like) are held by Apple and Google. Tensely neither accesses nor stores them.**

### 1.6 What we do not collect
We **do not** collect any of the following:
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
- Location data (GPS)
- Address book, photos, messages
- Camera access
- Audio recordings (the microphone is used only for real-time speech recognition — see 1.4)
- Health data
- Financial information (card details and the like)
- Browsing history

---

## 2. Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed only for the following purposes:

- Providing a personalised learning experience and recording your progress
- Account creation, sign-in and authentication
- Synchronising data across your devices
- Sending notifications and reminders (only if you granted permission)
- Subscription management
- Debugging, security and performance improvement
- Responding to support requests
- Meeting legal obligations

Your data is **not** used for **advertising**, **profiling** or **third-party
marketing**.

---

## 3. Legal Basis (KVKK Art. 5)

- **Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2/c):** managing your account and providing the service
- **Legal obligation (Art. 5/2/ç):** invoicing, financial records, consumer protection legislation
- **Legitimate interest (Art. 5/2/f):** debugging, security, fraud prevention
- **Explicit consent (Art. 5/1):** push notification preferences

---

## 4. Data Transfers

Your data is shared with the following service providers, each acting under its
own privacy policy:

| Provider | Purpose | Region |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (on AWS infrastructure) | Database, authentication | Frankfurt, Germany (EU) |
| **Apple Push Notification Service** | iOS notification delivery | Apple infrastructure |
| **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** | Android notification delivery | Google infrastructure |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription validation and management | United States |
| **Apple App Store / Google Play** | Payment processing | Apple / Google infrastructure |

Where transfers abroad are involved, the safeguards required by KVKK Art. 9
(standard contractual clauses) are in place.

We do **not** sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

---

## 5. Retention

- Learning records: for as long as the account is active
- After a deletion request: all your personal data is deleted or anonymised within **30 days at the latest**
- Data that must be retained by law (invoices and the like): for the period the legislation requires (5 years under Art. 253 of the Turkish Tax Procedure Law)

---

## 6. Your Rights Under KVKK

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

- Learn whether your personal data is being processed
- Request information if it has been processed
- Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly
- Know the third parties to whom your data has been transferred, at home or abroad
- Request correction if your data has been processed incompletely or inaccurately
- Request deletion or destruction under the conditions set out in KVKK Art. 7
- Object to an adverse outcome reached solely through automated analysis
- Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing

To exercise these rights: **info@tensemasteracademy.com**

We answer requests free of charge within **30 days at the latest**.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Tensely is an educational app for school-age learners. **Parental consent is
required for users under 13.**

- The sign-up screen states that accounts for users under 13 must be created with parental consent
- We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent
- Parents may request deletion of their child's data through the contact address above
- The app carries no advertising; no targeted advertising is shown

Account management through Apple Family Sharing is supported.

---

## 8. Security

- All communication is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text
- Payment details never reach Tensely's servers (they are processed by Apple and Google)
- Personal data is protected by Supabase Row-Level Security policies — each user can reach only their own data
- Subscription status is managed server-side (RevenueCat webhook) and cannot be altered by the user

Even so, no method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be
perfectly secure. If you become aware of any security incident, please tell us
immediately.

- Show an in-app notice for significant changes
- Email account holders

Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## 11. Deleting Your Account and Your Data

You can delete your account in two ways.

**From inside the app:** open **Profile → Delete my account** in Tensely. Your
account is deleted after two confirmation screens.

**By email:** if you cannot reach the app, send a deletion request to
**info@tensemasteracademy.com** from the email address registered to your
account. We complete such requests free of charge within **30 days at the
latest**.

Deletion removes:

- Your account details (email address, name, user identifier)
- Your learning records (completed lessons, mastery scores, streak, error analysis)
- Your notification preferences and push notification token
- Your subscription history is anonymised on Tensely's side

**What is not deleted:** invoices and financial records that must be retained for
5 years under Art. 253 of the Turkish Tax Procedure Law, and the purchase records
held by Apple and Google. To cancel your subscription you must also use your App
Store or Google Play subscription settings — deleting your account does not
cancel the subscription by itself.
